# Agent 3: 碎片表达与表达DNA (Expression DNA & Style)

## Overview

李文亚's expression style is his most distinctive and viral feature. It is a volatile compound of pseudo-academic lecturing, paranoid accusation, self-pity, messianic grandiosity, and explosive profanity. This document maps the full spectrum of his verbal behavior across approximately 30 subtitle files.

## 2. Sentence Patterns

### 2.1 The Explosive Short Burst (Rage Mode)

In confrontation mode, sentences fragment into 2-8 character barks:

> "你这个家伙"
> "你是个什么东西"
> "我日他"
> "你这个丑样子"
> "你很快会死"
> "我骂他"
> "他妈的"

**Example from 【民科TV】超级生气暴怒！裸身痛骂白锁成:**
> "妈的我再骂你妈的 / 我他妈的我他妈的 / 我吃了两碗饭 / 三个馒头 / 血糖没有控制好 / 他妈的 / 老子是高中毕业"

The staccato rhythm, sentence fragments, and repeated cursing create an almost Tourette's-like verbal explosion.

### 2.2 The Run-On Pseudo-Academic Sentence (Calm Theory Mode)

When explaining his "theories," sentences stretch into meandering, repetitive structures with no clear end:

> "太阳接触电感在固定一个位置 / 观察太阳接触电感来研究太阳地球的运动 / 研究地球的倾斜 / 这种照片主要是研究地球的运动和地球的倾斜"

> "地球和太阳互为参照物 / 你把太阳的运动现象给他看清楚 / 给他这个整个运动现象给他解释出来 / 把它解释出来 / 把这个现象给解释清楚 / 地球的运动明明白白的就摆在我们的面前"

These are characterized by:
- Extreme redundancy (saying the same thing 3-5 times with slight rephrasing)
- Circular reasoning (A proves B, B proves A)
- Filler words: "这个", "那个", "嗯", "啊", "就是说", "也就是", "也就是说"
- Never completing a logical chain

### 2.3 The Rhetorical Question Cascade

His signature is a barrage of rhetorical questions that demands agreement:

> "你公平吗 / 你抖音平台的做法做法对吗 / 你正确吗 / 你为什么不封住他的号"

> "他犯法不犯法 / 他犯罪不犯罪 / 该不该枪毙他们 / 你看他在这个单位能存在吗 / 你不该怀疑吗"

This pattern builds pseudo-logical momentum through quantity, not quality — the sheer number of questions overwhelms any single answer.

### 2.4 Declarative vs. Interrogative Ratio

- **Rage mode:** ~40% declarative accusations, ~40% rhetorical questions, ~20% profanity/interjections
- **Theory mode:** ~70% declarative (pseudo-explanations), ~20% rhetorical questions, ~10% filler
- **Defensive mode:** ~50% declarative (self-justification), ~30% questions, ~20% threats

Zero actual questions seeking information. Every question is prosecutorial.

This is a closed epistemic system — no external evidence can penetrate it because any contradictory evidence is by definition part of the persecution.

## 4. Rhetorical Devices

### 4.1 Repetition (The Hammer)

His most-used device. He repeats key phrases 3-8+ times per video:

> "你这些家伙们你这些家伙 / 你是个什么东西 / 首先我攻击你一句话 / 你是个什么东西"

> "我拍摄的太阳的照片那是很多的啊 / 原来拍摄太阳照片很多的 / 这些太阳照片 / 这些地方都是太阳的照片 / 主要是太阳照片"

Repetition serves as:
- Emotional escalation (each repeat more intense)
- Cognitive pressure (wear down listener)
- Self-reinforcement (convincing himself)

### 4.2 Threats and Ultimatums

The threat repertoire is extensive:

| Threat Type | Example |
|-------------|---------|
| **Execution** | "必须枪毙孙笑川" |
| **Institutional** | "必须把王楚淇开除出南京大学" |
| **Career** | "你把你的前途永远葬送了" |
| **Death prediction** | "我看你最后是怎么死的 / 你很快就会死亡" |
| **Historical oblivion** | "你一定被扫进历史的垃圾堆" |
| **Legal** | "该不该抓起来送进监狱" |
| **Cosmic** | "科学不能向前发展 / 人类不能进步" |

### 4.3 Appeal to Authority (Inverted)

Rather than appealing to credentialed authorities (which would exclude him), he:
1. **Appeals to "the people":** 中国人民, 世界人民, 人民群众
2. **Appeals to observation:** "我实际测出来的", "我亲眼观察到的"
3. **Appeals to himself as authority:** "我已经研究20多年"
4. **Reads fabricated supporter letters** from "professors" at parody institutions to create the appearance of academic backing

### 4.4 The Victim Narrative

He consistently frames himself as a persecuted genius:

> "一个没有上过大学的农民 / 我在搞科学研究超过20年 / 我抓紧我的每一分钟 / 我取得了重大的科学成果 / 疯狂打压"

> "他看不起农民 / 他看不起人民 / 他以为他上的学历高啊 / 他就要横行霸道 / 他就坚决地压制住没有上过大学的农民的科学成果"

The narrative arc:
1. I, an uneducated but brilliant man, discovered great truths
2. The establishment ("反动学术权威") suppresses me
3. They steal my ideas (孙笑川)
4. They control my computer, delete my videos
5. Despite persecution, I will triumph
6. All opponents will be executed or swept into history's dustbin

### 4.5 The List Device

He loves creating lists — hit lists, execution lists, institution lists:

> "南京大学的王书提要枪毙 / 中国人民大学陈人杰必须枪毙 / 武汉大学新批人小川必须枪毙 / 抗争气必须枪毙"

The list gives the impression of systematicity and completeness.

### 4.6 Reading Opponent's Bio Before Attacking

A hilarious recurring pattern: he reads the *full* academic biography of his opponent (often fabricated/parody), then immediately attacks:

> "缅甸医科大学附属医学院院长 / 医学博士 / 武汉大学医学医学院名誉教授 / 美国普林斯顿大学名誉教授 / 2015年诺贝尔生理医学奖提名者百所成教授教授... 他妈的"

### 4.7 The "Fake" Accusation

## 5. Emotional Range

The range spans from **calm, grandfatherly lecturing** to **screaming, half-naked rage** to **tearful self-pity** to **messianic grandiosity**.

### 5.1 Mode 1: Calm Pedantic Explainer (Temperature: Low)

In videos about his sun diameter theory, wire-pole measurement, or his biography, he speaks slowly, with many hesitations ("这个", "嗯"), explaining patiently:

> "这个照片是就是我在2020年时候的照片 / 这个照片呢和我现在的照片相比啊 / 那就说身体的嗯这个胖瘦程度是很搓的很圆的"

> "实上地球一天运动的距离要大于690 / 12000千米 / 就这个数字已经是太阳直径的 / 书本中的太阳直径的五倍"

In these segments, he sounds like a kindly old man showing you his photo album. The absurd content is delivered with utter sincerity.

### 5.2 Mode 2: Righteous Fury (Temperature: High)

Triggered by any criticism, especially from "professors":

> "我他妈的显微镜 / 你能观察气孔吗 / 显微镜必须治切片 / 放在显微镜里面才能观察"

> "你算个什么人物 / 你算什么教授 / 你是我龟教授"

The voice speeds up, profanity increases, sentences fragment, and threats emerge.

### 5.3 Mode 3: Persecution Paranoia (Temperature: Medium-High)

When discussing his stolen ideas, hacked computer, or suppressed videos:

> "这恶势力啊从没有停手 / 他们控制我的电脑 / 把我电脑里文章的内容各种删除"

> "我打开电脑是需要密码的 / 我现在点击这个电脑的平面 / 它不出现输入密码的位置"

This is delivered with a mix of anger and genuine bewilderment — he truly believes his computer is being remotely controlled by an academic mafia.

### 5.4 Mode 4: Tender Gratitude (Temperature: Low)

When reading praise from supporters:

> "可见这位网友啊 / 哼他给我的私信 / 他对我的评价之高 / 使我感动 / 我在这里向你表示感谢"

In these rare moments, his voice softens, he seems genuinely touched, and the profanity vanishes entirely.

### 5.5 Mode 5: Grandiose Prophecy (Temperature: Medium)

Declarations about the future of science and humanity:

> "我们活平均寿命高到120岁 / 160岁以上 / 平均寿命你至少能达到120岁"

> "我的星球的三恶人民范围太突出了 / 我的科技生活理论太突出了 / 我要的科学成果呀"

## 6. Narrative Framing

### 6.1 Self-Image: The People's Genius

He casts himself as:
- **"一个没有上过大学的农民"** — humble origins
- **"研究超过20年"** — dedicated, relentless
- **"我抓紧我的每一分钟"** — hardworking
- **"我取得了重大的科学成果"** — accomplished
- **"被疯狂打压"** — persecuted

The complete self-frame: *A self-taught peasant genius whose revolutionary discoveries threaten the academic establishment, so they persecute, censor, and steal from him.*

### 6.2 Opponent Framing: The Academic Criminal Class

Every opponent is slotted into one of these categories:

| Label | Example Targets | Function |
|-------|----------------|----------|
| **反动学术权威** | 王楚淇, 陈人杰, 浙江大学领导 | Makes them political enemies of the people |
| **恶势力** | 孙笑川 (primary), 抖音平台 | Criminalizes them as an underground mafia |
| **犯罪分子** | 碧桑驾dell, 刘浩文 (fake) | Demands legal punishment |
| **流氓/无赖** | 陈人杰, 白锁成 | Strips them of dignity |
| **旧理论的维护者** | All professors who disagree | Makes them obsolete |
| **书呆子** | 王楚淇 | Ridicules their education |

### 6.3 The Stolen Discovery Narrative

A key frame: his discoveries are constantly *stolen*:

> "这个孙笑川明明地偷走了我的理论"

> "他偷了他肯定急着发表 / 谁先发表就是谁的 / 那谁还敢去搞科学研究啊"

This serves multiple functions:
1. Explains why "credentialed" scientists have similar ideas
2. Generates sympathy (he's the victim)
3. Justifies his rage (righteous anger at theft)
4. Elevates his status (if they steal it, it must be valuable)

### 6.4 The Good vs. Evil Frame

The moral landscape is starkly Manichaean:
- **His side:** 人民, 人民群众, 支持者, 真正的科学家, 有良知的教授 (北京大学校长支持他)
- **Their side:** 恶势力, 反动势力, 犯罪集团, 流氓无赖, 旧理论的维护者

There is no neutral ground. You are either with him (supporting truth and progress) or against him (supporting crime and scientific stagnation).

## 9. Structural Patterns Across a Typical Video

A typical Li Wenya video follows this structure:

1. **Hook** (0-15s): Direct address to opponent or platform. Often a curse or accusation.
2. **Context** (15s-2min): Reads a "supporter letter" or "opponent's message" from a fan.
3. **Emotional escalation** (2-5min): Reacts to the content. Goes from calm reading to agitated commentary.
4. **Theory exposition** (if applicable): Explains his science, often with diagrams or photos.
5. **Rage climax**: Peaks with execution calls, threats, profanity.
6. **Closing threat**: "不信咱们走着瞧" or "一定被扫进历史的垃圾堆."

This pattern makes his content predictable yet watchable — the viewer knows each video will escalate.

## 10. Synthesis: The Expression DNA

Li Wenya's expression style can be understood as the collision of:

- **A high school science teacher's vocabulary** (he taught biology for 16 years) — attempts at formal scientific language
- **A street vendor's directness** (flower seller, factory worker) — profanity and bluntness
- **A paranoid's epistemology** — every disagreement is persecution, everything is connected
- **An autodidact's confidence** — no formal training, therefore no awareness of what he doesn't know
- **A Cultural Revolution-era political vocabulary** — "反动", "人民", "枪毙", "封建" as the frame for all conflict
- **An elderly man's speech patterns** — repetition, filler words, slower pace, circuitous sentences

The result is a uniquely unstable and volatile expression system: he can flip from grandfatherly pedagogue to screaming executioner in a single sentence, and this unpredictability is a core part of his entertainment value. His sincerity is absolute — he is not performing, which makes every absurd claim, every death threat, every half-naked rant genuinely and unsettlingly real.
