# Agent 2: Dialogue & Improvisational Thinking — 对话与即兴思考

李文亚's conversational patterns as observed from interview-style parody videos and monologue responses. All sources are AI-translated Chinese SRT subtitles from the **民科TV** / **新民科TV** / **文亚回忆录** accounts. These are parody productions, but the dialogue attributed to 李文亚 follows a consistent internal logic that reveals his rhetorical and cognitive habits.

**Context note:** "Interviews" are conducted by parody journalists (诺委会/Nobel Committee, 某大学记者/university reporter, 文亚回忆录/memoirist). "Monologues" are his direct-to-camera video responses. Both serve the parody genre but the thinking patterns displayed are consistent.

## 1. Defensive Patterns When Challenged

### 1a. Immediate Dismissal via "Evil Forces" (恶势力) Framing

The single most consistent pattern: when confronted with criticism,李文亚 reframes the critic as belonging to an organized evil conspiracy trying to suppress his truth.

> "饿死你到他死亡的时候了，他这个疯狂的这个反扑，那个疯狗咬人那招八开头发疯的时候呢" (回归专访)
> — When told that "evil forces" are attacking him harder after his return.

> "他是恶势力，他一直都在诋毁您呢"
> — Interviewer prompts, and Li immediately agrees: the critic is part of the evil forces.

> "那一定是AI制作的视频"
> — When shown a video of his arch-nemesis 孙笑川 standing on his back. (健康采访)

> "这个视频呢也就是说说明他们的本性是难改的，这些人呢是不会迷途知返的" (回归专访)
> — Dismissing a critical video as evil forces' work.

**Pattern:** Any criticism = evidence of an organized conspiracy. This framing is *pre-emptive* — it positions the speaker as unable to be rationally refuted because the opposition is not acting in good faith.

### 1b. Immediate Refusal / Walk-Away Threat

Under sustained questioning, 李文亚's most extreme defensive move is to threaten termination:

> "你不要跟他提这些问题！这些我肯定我之前我哥就看了，你给我取消！我那不行，这个算了，他提的问题你就给我取消了！你相信他就给我取消算了，我不要了，我不要这个东西了不行！对你你不要跟跟跟我纠缠" (诺委会完整版)
> — When pressed on molecular volume vs molecular size. After two minutes of circling, he demands the conversation be terminated.

> "你跟我说了，你说的是相反的... 我现在再跟你说这个问题，我们就没法说了。真的我对我感觉到是这样的" (诺委会完整版)
> — He declares the conversation impossible when the same question persists.

### 1c. Reversal: "You Lied" / "They Misrepresented Me"

When faced with a claim he allegedly made that is being criticized, he denies ever saying it:

> (Interviewer: "He said you believe molecular volume and molecular size are the same thing")
> "他说的这不是我说的！而不是一个东西！我说是个鬼一个东西，你说的刚好相反不" (诺委会完整版)

> "他那他骗我了，他的原话是您认为这两个是一个东西"
> — Interviewer pushes back, but Li maintains the denial.

> "他说的真不是我说的，这不是一个东西，我说是个鬼一个东西，你说的刚好相反" (诺委会音频流出)
> — Same incident, same denial.

**Pattern:** He never acknowledges inconsistency. His strategy is binary: "I never said that" or "they twisted my words." This keeps his internal framework intact — he can be consistent because he *defines* what he said in the moment.

### 1d. Escalation to Physical Threat / Profanity

When frustration peaks, threats of violence emerge:

> "我跟他当面骂他打他，我日他结巴" (诺委会完整版)

> "他跟我抖什么弄" ... "我在这里干嘛的" (诺委会完整版)
> — Responding to a critic he cannot reach in person.

> "放他妈了个屁！我了个逼！这个王世猫王维横啊，一定是造四的！" (回归专访)
> — Explosive reaction to 王伟恒 suggesting he try 白体生物饮料.

> "这家伙必须得枪毙掉" (repeated across multiple interviews — a signature rhetorical escalation)

> "如果还有白体生物原料，这个生物饮料之类，一定是该枪毙的" (回归专访)
> — The execution demand applied to products, not just people.

**Pattern:** The progression is consistent across contexts: **calm explanation → irritation → denial/reversal → profanity → physical threats**. The switch can happen within seconds.

### 1e. The "You Haven't Understood" Defense

When unable to refute a specific criticism on its merits, he attributes the failure to the critic's lack of comprehension:

> "你那那就说明你是你还没有看懂，他就这么厉害，他就是这么厉害，就是啊比较惊心动魄啊" (回归专访)
> — When the interviewer expresses concern about his health videos.

> "我在这里说个实在话...由于我这个百年以来他那个理论呢啊都是那样，我研究基础国和平和他们的意林，多方面是推翻的，也就是说他是错误的" (大学记者采访)
> — Before explaining why critics don't understand.

## 2. Position Changes: Rare but Instructional

李文亚 almost never changes core positions, but small adjustments happen. They follow a pattern: **he never admits error; he redefines.**

### 2a. Black Body Biological Classification

> "我这个得这个黑体微生物，黑体生物我开始叫微生物，后来我就叫做生物，因为它是介于微生物和动物之间的生物，它现在改改变成它不属于微生物，因为它在我人体的这个重量可以达到几公斤，这是我实实在在的对个人的研究" (豆包引用北大)

He acknowledges changing "微生物" (microorganism) to "生物" (organism), but frames it as progressive refinement, not error correction. The justification ("it weighs several kg") explains *why* the term changed.

### 2b. Detection Claim Shift

In one interview, he says black body organisms cannot be detected; in another, he insists they can:

> "我说这个黑体生物不可检测，不能够检测到，我说这个东西它实实在在很难检测，但是不是不能检测" (大学记者采访)
> — A mid-sentence reversal from "cannot detect" to "can detect but it's hard."

> "他是可以一定是可以检测的，一定可以检测到的" (same interview, moments later)
> — Full reversal into certainty.

**The pivot mechanism:** "很难检测" (hard to detect) acts as a bridging phrase that lets him shift positions mid-utterance without acknowledging contradiction.

### 2c. Healing Claims: Simultaneous Certainty and Uncertainty

> "我全治愈了这个疾病，这个疾病在我身上已经不存在了" (健康采访)

> "我不能说结果，我不能说我一定能够使我的寿命延长的很长...我没有取得真正的实际的有用的有效的" (豆包引用北大)
> — Just minutes or videos apart, he claims both complete victory and ongoing struggle.

**Pattern:** The same man who claims total cure also admits he hasn't fully eliminated the organisms. These coexist because his "battle" narrative allows for *strategic* claims — total cure for propagandistic effect, partial progress for scientific caution.

### 2d. Sun Diameter: Mutating Numbers

> "太阳直径是地球的33万倍，太阳的直径就是42亿公里" (太阳直径争议)

He previously claimed 19亿公里 in other videos. He does not explain the 19→42 shift; he simply states the new number with the same absolute certainty.

## 3. Analogies Used in Real-Time

His analogies draw from everyday experience, not scientific literature:

### 3a. Airplane Distance Analogy (for Sun Diameter)

> "我们天空中的一个飞机啊，下面它几10米长，但是他到几万公里的时候，就是我们拳头大小，你就是看见他就很小了。而我们离我们几公里的太阳，看到的和月球的大小一般大，这个太阳的直径体积质量一定是很大的" (太阳直径争议)

This is his signature observational argument: if things look smaller at distance, then the sun MUST be huge because it looks moon-sized from 1 AU away. The analogy collapses if you understand angular diameter, but it feels intuitive.

### 3b. Hydrogen vs Oxygen Molecules

> "你比如说氢气，它是一种气体，它只有两个氢原子...两氧气虽然是两个氧原子，但是这两个每个氧原子它都有12个电子...12个电子跟你一个电子，这个狄青一样，它的分子的直径是一样吗？" (诺委会完整版)

He uses atomic electron count to argue molecular size varies — a layperson's intuition. He then calls opposing views "完全是混蛋逻辑" (completely bullshit logic).

### 3c. Nutrition Parallel: Black Bodies Eat Vitamins Too

> "我们都知道这个维生素它是这个人体需要的营养物质...它是生物，他就也会需要维生素，也会需要维生维生素" (豆包引用北大)

He reasons: humans eat vitamins, so black body organisms must also consume them. This is used to explain why vitamin C therapy doesn't cure the condition — the organisms eat the vitamins too.

### 3d. Dog Biting as Opponent Metaphor

> "那个疯狗咬人那招八开头发疯的时候呢" (回归专访)
> — Comparing his critics to rabid dogs.

### 3e. Construction Material Analogy

> "你那些恶势微乎周礼的家伙，让你继续攻击" (太阳直径争议)
> — Dismissing critics as trivial/nuisance-level.

## 5. Self-Narrative: Framing His Life Story

His life is told as a **persecuted genius narrative** with four stages:

### 5a. Tragic Childhood (Pre-Existing Victimhood)

> "我的童年是悲痛的，我的家庭是一个地主成分...我的童年是在被别人鄙视欺负，欺负侮辱的情况下成长起来的" (深度访谈下)

> "我们在这个社会上是一个很低等的人，家到哪里，哪里人都可以欺负你" (深度访谈下)

The "landlord class" background during Maoist China gives him a foundational identity as a victim — which maps directly onto his current self-image as a persecuted scientist.

### 5b. Lucky Failure (Failed Exams as Necessary)

> "如果我考上了正规大学，考上了重点大学，就没有我的科学发现，就没有我的科学研究，是我的研究成果" (深度访谈下)

His failure to enter university is reframed as **necessary suffering** that enabled his genius. This is crucial: it converts a source of shame into a badge of honor.

### 5c. Mercantile Success (Proof of Practical Intelligence)

He proved his ability in the real world. This serves as evidence against the charge that he's merely uneducated — he *chose* research over money.

### 5d. Moral Scientist (Selfless Seeker of Truth)

## 6. Reconciling Contradictions: Cognitive Mechanisms

李文亚 has developed several techniques that allow contradictory statements to coexist without cognitive dissonance:

### 6a. The "Gradual Progress" Escape Hatch

When cornered on a failed prediction (e.g., his healing claims), he shifts to process:

> "我不能说结果，我不能说我一定能够使我的寿命延长的很长...因为我对我自己身体的这种介于微生物和动物之间的生物没有消灭掉，没有完全消灭掉，我一定是消灭掉的一部分，我没有取得真正的实际的有用的有效的" (豆包引用北大)

**Contradiction resolved by:** moving from "done" to "in progress." The past victory claim and current incompleteness coexist because science (in his framing) is always provisional.

### 6b. "Good Faith vs Bad Faith" Dichotomy

He splits all critics into two categories: those who engage in good faith (whom he will accept correction from) and those who are "evil forces" (whom he will fight):

> "真正拿不过的错误来进行进行批评，再进行说理，那我错了，我就应该承认错误，一个人嘛，你不可能是哪个地方都正确" (大学记者采访)
> — A rare statement of openness... that is never actually demonstrated.

> "但是一部分人他是恶的攻击，他是跟你不讲道理的，他就是你的学历低，你没有那么高的学历，他就你不坚决的否定你的一切成一切成果" (大学记者采访)
> — The condition that excludes all actual critics.

**Pattern:** The "good faith critic" is a theoretical construct that never exists in practice. Every real critic is reclassified as evil forces.

### 6c. The Non-Detection Escape

When asked why nobody can observe black body organisms:

> "我不需要什么标本，我把它消灭就行" (健康采访)
> "我不需要影像，不需要标本，不需要检测，我把这小麦掉了就行" (健康采访)

**Logic:** The proof of existence is the *act of eliminating* it. The absence of evidence becomes evidence through action.

> "对于我来说，我不可能把我的哪个地方解剖开，用显微镜给他观察观察，这是这是不可能的。但是他是不是不可检测的，是能够检测的" (大学记者采访)

Undetectable by him → possible for others. The contradiction of "can't detect" and "can detect" is bridged by shifting the agency: *he* can't (won't) do it, but science could.

### 6d. The Fictional Proof

When presented with praise from obviously fictional institutions, he treats them as real validation:

Reading a letter from "东京大学术物物理系" (a parody institution):
> "这个他得说到我的健康问题啊啊这里我需要说一点...这个自从治疗我自己治疗我的皮肤病以来" (新民科TV读支持者信)

He selectively extracts the parts he wants (health advice from the "professor") and ignores the clear parody framing.

### 6e. Retrospective Reinterpretation

When his own past statements are cited against him:

> "他说您认为分子体积跟分子大小是一回事" (interviewer)
> "他说的不是我说的！而不是一个东西！我说是个鬼一个东西，你说的刚好相反不" (诺委会)

He either: (a) flatly denies, (b) claims misquotation, or (c) insists the other person deliberately twisted his words. He never *reconsiders* — he *rejects the premise*.

## 7. Conversational Flow Patterns

Based on observing multiple full-length interactions:

### In Interview Mode (with 诺委会/记者)

```
[Interviewer question] 
→ Li: brief framing/context (说个实在话...)
→ Li: dismisses premise (that person is evil/misled)
→ Li: states his position (absolute certainty)
→ [Interviewer follow-up with contradiction]
→ Li: "你说的是相反的" / "你还没看懂"
→ [Interviewer persists]
→ Li: profanity / threat / walk-away
```

**Real example (诺委会完整版):**
1. Interviewer: "Why do you say molecular volume = molecular size?"
2. Li: "He's a liar, I never said that."
3. Interviewer: "But he says you did."
4. Li: "You believe him? Then cancel this interview! I don't need it!"
5. Interviewer: "No no, we believe you—"
6. Li: "Don't bring up those questions!"

### In Monologue Mode (his own videos)

```
→ Li: announces a discovery (big claim)
→ Li: attacks evil forces who deny it
→ Li: mentions he's being persecuted
→ Li: makes prediction/threat against enemies
→ Li: promises future proof he will produce
```

### Cross-Interview Consistency of Key Phrases

- "说个实在话" — his most frequent conversational opener (lit. "to tell the truth") — used before every major claim.
- "实实在在的" / "实实在在" — assertion of authenticity, used 5-10x per interview.
- "这家伙必须枪毙" — the execution demand, his ultimate rhetorical weapon.
- "你还没有看懂" — dismissal of inadequate comprehension.

## Summary: The Conversational李文亚

| Dimension | Pattern |
|---|---|
| **When challenged** | Classifies challenger as "evil forces," then attacks, threatens, or walks away |
| **Position changes** | Never admits error; redefines terms or shifts frames; small terminology changes okay, never conceptual |
| **Analogies** | Everyday physics (airplanes, buckets, vitamins); never abstract math |
| **Refused questions** | His healing method, exam scores, hacking details, IQ — anything that could be falsified |
| **Life narrative** | Persecuted genius: tragic past → necessary failure → worldly success → selfless science |
| **Contradictions** | Reconciled via: temporal splitting (now vs later), agency shifting (I can't but science can), categorical dismissal (they're bad faith), or flat denial (I never said that) |

His conversational style is **ritualistic** — it follows a predictable script regardless of topic. The same defense sequence, the same denials, the same metaphors recur across different interviews with different "journalists." What appears as improvisation is, in fact, a tightly rehearsed repertoire of rhetorical moves evolved over 20+ years of arguing against everyone who questions him.
