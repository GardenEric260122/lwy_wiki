## KEY UNCERTAINTIES & DISCREPANCIES

1. **Start date of research**: 2008 (external doc) vs. 2005 (extrapolating from "13 years by 2018"). Li himself says "研究就研究了20多年" (researched for 20+ years) — this is clearly an overstatement or rounding.

2. **Birth date**: No official documentation. Self-reported as ~1954. One source says "1954年1月" (January 1954).

3. **Financial details**: Claim of single payment for Shenzhen apartment vs. the documentary's "卖房搞民科" (selling house for folk science) narrative are contradictory. He may have sold property in Henan, not his primary residence.

4. **Medical claims**: No independent verification of his diabetes cure claims. Viewers noted visible weight loss in before/after comparisons, consistent with untreated diabetes.

5. **Mental health**: Multiple external documentaries suggest possible schizophrenia-spectrum disorder, delusional disorder, or paraphrenia. No formal diagnosis is available. His behavior (extreme paranoia, fixed false beliefs, lack of insight) is consistent with serious mental illness.

6. **Timeline of internet entry**: When exactly he first started posting on Douyin is unclear — estimates range from 2020 to 2023.

7. **Real name**: Even "李文亚" may not be his real name. The parody universe uses "李维亚" interchangeably, and no official identification documents have been produced.
